# DarkFrame visual collections

DarkFrame is not dark-only. Every collection uses the same optical grid and app identity but can use a different finish.

## Noir
Luxury monochrome: black tile, warm-white glyph, subtle keyline.

## Color Pop
Pixel/iOS-inspired clarity: near-black soft tile with the app's recognizable brand colors. Clean, saturated, never neon.

## Frost
Light premium: off-white/frosted tile, graphite glyphs, restrained depth.

## Titanium
Neutral metallic graphite/silver, monochrome glyphs, subtle highlights.

## Glass
Dark or light translucent-looking surfaces with restrained highlights. No excessive gradients.

## Pure AMOLED
True black, simplified high-contrast glyphs.

Rules: consistent 512 grid, 60% optical safe area, consistent corner geometry, no copying third-party icon-pack artwork, and every variant must remain recognizable at launcher size.
