# DarkFrame Design System — v0.1

## Classic Outline
- Canvas: 512 × 512.
- Tile: rounded square, optical radius 22%, near-black (#090B0E).
- Safe glyph area: 58–62% of canvas.
- Glyph: warm white (#F4F4F4), visually consistent stroke weight.
- Border: subtle warm-white keyline; never copy another pack's geometry.
- Optical centering beats mathematical centering.
- No gradients in Classic Outline.
- Every icon must remain legible at 48 px.

## Collections
1. Classic Outline — launch style.
2. AMOLED — pure-black, borderless.
3. Glass — restrained translucent treatment.
4. Chrome — monochrome metallic treatment.

All artwork must be original. Brand glyphs may reference recognizable trademarks only as needed to identify the corresponding app; do not reuse third-party icon-pack artwork.
