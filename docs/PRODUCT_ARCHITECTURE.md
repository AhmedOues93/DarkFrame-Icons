# DarkFrame product architecture

DarkFrame remains an icon-first, offline-first Android customization product.

## Content
Icons, wallpapers, widgets and complete styles are local catalog items. Collections are data-driven so later packs do not require navigation rewrites.

## Monetization
FREE and PRO are domain-level tiers. V1 defaults to FREE until a real Google Play Billing entitlement provider is wired. No fake purchases.

## Android boundaries
DarkFrame never promises silent system customization. Wallpaper APIs and launcher-supported icon application can be automated only where Android permits; restricted changes use guided setup.

## Fold
Phone, cover-screen and expanded layouts must be validated independently. Large screens get adapted content density rather than stretched phone UI.
